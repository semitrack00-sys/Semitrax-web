Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Semi-Trax (“Semi-Trax,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our websites, mobile applications, navigation tools, pilot programs, and related services (the “Services”).

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information, including your name, email, telephone number, company, fleet, and login information.
  • Vehicle and route information, including truck dimensions, weight, axles, trailer type, hazmat settings, destinations, preferences, and trip history.
  • Precise GPS location, direction, speed, and route progress when location features are enabled.
  • Device identifiers, IP address, operating system, usage activity, crash logs, and diagnostics.
  • Communications, support requests, pilot feedback, and transaction information.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide truck-aware routing and navigation, personalize vehicle profiles, calculate routes and arrival times, display relevant stops and alerts, operate pilot programs, secure and improve the Services, communicate with users, process transactions, comply with law, and protect users and the public.

3. Location choices

You can control location permissions through your device. Disabling precise location may prevent navigation, live route progress, arrival estimates, and safety alerts from working correctly.

4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to providers supporting hosting, analytics, customer service, payments, mapping, routing, traffic, communications, and security; to your fleet when using an organization-managed account; during a business transaction; when required by law; or to protect safety and rights. We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Third-party services

Third-party maps, traffic data, road information, payment services, fleet systems, ELD platforms, and linked websites may process information under their own privacy policies. Semi-Trax is not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Retention and security

We keep information as reasonably necessary to provide the Services and meet legal and business obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of certain information and opt out of promotional messages. Additional rights may apply depending on your location. We may verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Children

The Services are intended for commercial drivers, owner-operators, fleets, and adults, not children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the Services develop. The revised effective date will appear above, and material changes may be communicated through the Services or by email.

10. Contact

Contact contact@semitrax.com. Semi-Trax is based in Nevada, United States.
