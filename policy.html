Semi-Trax is designed primarily to assist commercial truck drivers with navigation and trip planning. Depending on availability, location, device, subscription level, and third-party services, features may include commercial-truck GPS navigation, turn-by-turn directions, voice guidance, truck-specific routing, route recalculation, multiple-stop routing, vehicle profiles, height/weight/width/length/axle restrictions, hazmat routing, low-clearance warnings, bridge and tunnel information, road restrictions, traffic, closures, construction, truck stops, rest areas, weigh stations, parking, fuel, repair facilities, truck washes, restaurants, hotels, stores, cameras, weather, hours-of-service assistance, ELD integrations, and other trucking/navigation services.
